Android Auto fixes for the Messages screen.

Direct Messages are listed again. The car screen shows a limited number of rows, and on a radio with several channels the channels used every available row, so the Direct Messages section disappeared completely. Channels and direct messages now share the space.

The Messages screen no longer says there is nothing to show while it is still loading. It briefly reported no channels and no messages even with a radio connected and messages waiting.
